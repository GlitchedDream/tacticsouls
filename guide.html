Quick overview

The Gods of Ruin have enslaved the Ghost Kids, with their cult the Ember Oath burning the tribal lands. Three friends set out to save the Ghosts. They find allies on the road, search for the lost Soul tribe, and travel to each tribe's land until they reach the Temple of Lost Souls.

🎯 The goal

Win all 69 story battles across 7 chapters. Grow your squad, level it up, then climb the Leaderboard as a Warlord.

🧩 Your warband

Start with a Hoplite, a Hunter and an Oracle. Up to 8 warriors, plus a 9th slot for your own Lost Soul after the story ends.

🗺️ Game modes

Story · Battle (AI squads on Easy, Normal or Elite) · Outpost · Leaderboard · Market · PVP. Each tile appears once you unlock it.

Your road: what unlocks when

How a turn plays out

  1. The fastest fighter goes first. Everyone fills a turn meter by their ⚡ Speed; the first to 100 acts.
  2. Move up to your 👣 Move in tiles. You can climb a step only as high as your Jump.
  3. Act once: attack, cast, heal or use a skill. Pick a target and see the damage and hit chance first.
  4. End your turn and pick which way to face. Hits from the side and back hurt more.
  5. Skip moving or acting and your next turn comes sooner. You also get back 1 💧 MP each turn.
💡 Tip: Rival levels are fixed. They never scale to your squad, so extra Battle mode fights always make the story easier.

Core mechanics

The rules behind every fight, straight from the game's code.

⚔️ Damage calculator

💥 How damage works

Physical: (Atk × power × 1.5 + 1 − Def × 0.6), then × facing, × height, rounded. Never below 1.

Magic: Mag × power × 1.5 + 1 − Res × 0.5. Always hits and ignores facing and height.

Shield Wall cuts physical damage by 20% for allies within 3 tiles of a Hoplite who has it.

🏔️ Height

  • +5% damage per level you stand above the target, −5% per level below. Capped at ±15%.
  • Melee can't reach a target more than 2 levels up or down.
  • Bows get +1 range when you stand 2+ levels above.
  • Melee reaches all 8 tiles around you, diagonals included.

⏱️ Turn order & MP

  • Each tick, every fighter adds its Speed to a meter. At 100 it takes a turn.
  • Moving costs 50, acting costs 50. Skipping either costs only 30.
  • Every turn starts with +1 MP. Skills cost 2 to 4 MP.
  • Your captain gets +1 to its main stat and +1 Speed.

🌀 Status effects

📈 XP & Obols

  • XP per action: hit a foe +2, KO +4 more, heal +2, Safety Shield +1.
  • End of battle: win +6 (+3 if KO'd), loss +2. Story replays give 1/3 XP.
  • Next level needs 8 + 4 × your level in XP.
  • Story win +250 , loss −50. Battle mode win: Easy 100, Normal 150, Elite 250.

Ranks

Every fighter for hire at the Outpost has a rank. The Outpost offers 3 fighters a day near your squad's average level. One of them gets the day's best roll.

💡 Tip: An Elite fighter is always shiny, in gold. The day's best roll is Elite only 1 time in 100.

🏆 Warlord ranking

Every player is a Warlord. The Leaderboard sorts by furthest Story battle won, then PVP wins, then fewest PVP losses. Battle mode wins and losses stay private; PVP records are public.

Badges

There are no badges in Tactic Souls yet. Your milestones live on the road instead: recruits, new modes and your Lost Soul.
